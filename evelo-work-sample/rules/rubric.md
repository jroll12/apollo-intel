# Editor rubric (score each criterion 1-5)

Reference voice (5 phrases quoted from the brand's own website):
1. "Market statistics provide the starting point, not the complete answer."
2. "Selling your home shouldn't be stressful. Let us handle the process for you."
3. "The right strategy starts with current local data and a clear understanding of your goals."
4. "You should never feel like you're navigating an important decision alone."
5. "Making a Difference. Changing Lives. Leaving a Legacy."

| Criterion | 5 means | 1 means |
|---|---|---|
| HOOK | The first line earns the next one; specific, curiosity or value up front | Generic opener ("Happy Monday!", "Are you thinking of buying?") |
| CLARITY | One idea, plain English, a 9th-grade reading level | Several ideas, jargon, hard to skim |
| LOCAL | Names real towns, data or details from Central Indiana | Could be posted by any agent anywhere |
| CTA | One low-pressure, concrete next step that fits the channel | No CTA, several CTAs, or a pushy one |
| VOICE | Sounds like the reference phrases: calm, helpful, data-first, warm | Hype, salesy, or robotic |

Pass: every criterion scores 4 or higher. Revise: any criterion scores 3 or lower. Give one sentence of fix guidance per criterion scoring 3 or lower.
