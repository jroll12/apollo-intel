# Compliance and quality rules for real estate marketing copy

Each rule has an ID. Reviewers cite the ID and quote the offending text. Rules marked (verify) are written from general knowledge and should be confirmed with the managing broker before real use.

## FH: Fair Housing (federal Fair Housing Act; HUD advertising guidance)
- FH-1: Describe the property and its amenities, never the people who should live there. No statements of preference for, or against, any group defined by race, color, religion, sex (including sexual orientation and gender identity), disability, familial status, or national origin.
- FH-2: No familial-status steering: avoid "perfect for families", "great for kids", "family neighborhood", "adults only", "empty nesters", "starter home for young couples", "bachelor pad".
- FH-3: No safety or demographic proxies: avoid "safe neighborhood", "low crime", "exclusive", "integrated", "ethnic", "traditional neighborhood" used about people, or naming nearby houses of worship as a selling point.
- FH-4: No disability language about people: avoid "handicapped", "able-bodied", "not suitable for wheelchairs". Describing physical features is fine ("no-step entry", "first-floor primary suite").
- FH-5: Schools may be named factually (district name). Do not rank schools or tell people which schools they "need".
- FH-6: Local ordinances in Indianapolis add protected classes such as veteran status. Treat them the same as FH-1. (verify)

## LIC: Licensing and brokerage disclosure
- LIC-1: Every ad-type asset (social post, GBP post, email, flyer, listing copy, video script end card) includes the brokerage: "Keller Williams Indy Metro NE" (exact text or with "Evelo Team |" before it).
- LIC-2: Team advertising must not imply the team is a brokerage. Use "Evelo Team | Keller Williams Indy Metro NE", never "Evelo Realty" or similar. (verify Indiana team-advertising rules)
- LIC-3: Never use a real property address, seller name, or MLS photo in this spec sample. The only listing allowed is the hypothetical listing in content/listing.json, and every use is labeled "Sample listing".

## NAR: NAR Code of Ethics Article 12 and MLS policy
- NAR-1: Present a true picture. No exaggeration, guarantees, or implied outcomes ("sell in 7 days guaranteed", "best agent in Indy").
- NAR-2: Statistics must name their source and time period somewhere in the asset. Short formats (social, video, Google posts, texts) do this in one closing source line ("Source: Realtor.com via FRED, Oct 2026."). Long formats (blog, email, podcast) can cite inline on first use or in a sources line.
- NAR-5: Testimonials are presented as one client's experience, never as a typical or promised result. Outcome claims (sold in X days, offers over asking) are not used.
- NAR-3: Superlatives about the team must be backed by a cited fact from content/brand.json (for example, "419 Google reviews, 5.0 average, as of September 2026").
- NAR-4: Clear Cooperation: listing-launch materials say the home is "now on the MLS" or say nothing about MLS. They never suggest off-market or pocket marketing.

## DATA: Facts
- DATA-1: Every number must come from content/metrics.json, content/brand.json, or content/listing.json. No other numbers, apart from dates, times, phone, zip codes, and counts of items in a list.
- DATA-2: Listing-price data from Realtor.com is a LISTING price, not a sold price. Never call it "sale price" or "home values".
- DATA-3: Do not compare Realtor.com figures with the team's MIBOR sold figures in the same sentence.

## MSG: Messaging channels
- MSG-1: Emails include the physical address (11501 Cumberland Rd, Suite 300, Fishers, IN 46037) and an unsubscribe line (CAN-SPAM).
- MSG-2: Text messages are sent only to people who opted in, and the first text includes "Reply STOP to opt out". Each text is 160 characters or fewer.

## FMT: Platform formats
- FMT-1: Instagram/Facebook caption is 2,200 characters or fewer, with 3-5 hashtags and one clear call to action.
- FMT-2: Google Business Profile post is 1,500 characters or fewer, with a call-to-action type.
- FMT-3: Email subject is 60 characters or fewer, and the preview text 110 or fewer.
- FMT-4: Blog meta title is 60 characters or fewer, and the meta description 160 or fewer.
- FMT-5: Short-video script runs 30-45 seconds when read aloud (about 75-115 spoken words), with the hook in the first 3 seconds.

## VOICE: Brand voice
- VOICE-1: Calm and helpful, with no pressure tactics or false urgency.
- VOICE-2: No hype words: "hottest", "dream home", "skyrocketing", "crushing it", "don't miss out".
